Logistics Safety

Never send money to a transporter before checking their identity, vehicle information and reputation.

FARMERS will eventually introduce transporter verification, user ratings, secure communication and transaction protection.

How FARMERS Logistics Works

1. Create Request

Tell the network what you need transported and where it needs to go.

2. Find Transport

The future online system will search for suitable transport providers.

3. Connect

Farmers and transport providers can communicate before accepting a job.

4. Rate

After a completed delivery, users can rate their experience.

✓ Future Transporter Verification

Transport providers on the future FARMERS network can be required to verify their identity and provide relevant vehicle or business details.

A verification badge should only appear after FARMERS has actually completed the verification process.
